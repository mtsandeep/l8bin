Every time I wanted to show someone a demo, it was already gone — killed to free up RAM for the next one. One cheap VPS meant one app. Cloud free tiers work until you hit their framework limits or your app gets purged for inactivity. Existing self-hosted platforms are built for always-on production workloads. Nothing fit what I needed.

So I built LiteBin — entire stack under 58MB. Push from your laptop, no building on the VPS. Apps sleep to zero when idle, wake on the first request. Deploy as many as your disk holds.

Ship it your way.

One command from any directory. No YAML, no CI config, no Dockerfile needed. Builds happen on your machine. The VPS just runs the result.

  • ✔Builds on your laptop, zero CPU pressure on the VPS
  • ✔Streams the image directly: no registry, no Docker Hub
  • ✔Auto-detects Node, Python, Go, PHP and more via Railpack

Connect your GitHub account once. LiteBin creates the App, writes the deploy workflow into your repo, and sets the secrets itself. Push to deploy.

  • ✔One-click GitHub App setup, no review process, you own it
  • ✔Workflow + secrets written for you, nothing to wire by hand
  • ✔Builds run on GitHub-hosted runners; deploy on push or on demand

Add the LiteBin MCP server to Cursor, Claude Code, or any MCP-capable editor. Your agent pairs once, then deploys, verifies, sets env and restarts, end to end. No dashboard visits, no SSH.

  • ✔One entry in your editor's MCP settings; l8b init --mcp writes it for you
  • ✔Scoped tokens: the agent gets exactly the access you approve
  • ✔Machine-readable everything: deploy → health-check → logs → fix → redeploy

All three paths authenticate the same way. No SSH keys on your CI or your agent.

~/my-app

The situations that led to LiteBin.

The link you shared is already dead

You demoed something on a free tier and moved on. Weeks later, someone actually clicks the link — purged for inactivity. Apps here sleep, they don't disappear. The URL keeps working; it just wakes on the first hit.

Too many ideas, one small VPS

Every experiment deserves a URL, but RAM says otherwise — so you keep deleting the old to make room for the new. Idle apps cost nothing here. Stop choosing which project gets to exist.

Your agent built it. Where does it live?

You build in Cursor or Claude, but shipping still means a dashboard, a card on file, a per-app plan. Give the agent a scoped token instead: it deploys, health-checks, reads logs and redeploys while you stay in the editor.

The box already has a job

The VPS runs your stuff; it doesn't need a 500MB+ tenant moving in. LiteBin's whole stack rests under 58MB, manages what's already there, and when one box isn't enough you connect more from the same dashboard.

You like building, not maintaining

The client site you shipped last year? The URL is theirs now — redesigned, redirected, or gone one day. Keep your own copies on your VPS instead: a living archive of your freelance work, where idle copies cost nothing and wake the moment someone clicks.

One Manager.
Unlimited Bins.

Manage a fleet of tiny droplets from a single command center. LiteBin uses mTLS to secure communication between your Manager and Agents.

  • ✔Mix any VPS, any provider: a 512MB box from Hetzner, a spare DigitalOcean droplet, an old home server. Each server hosts its own set of apps; more servers means room for more projects.
  • ✔Remote control: restart, reload, and update any app from a single dashboard.
  • ✔Auto-placement: new apps are dropped onto the server with the most free capacity at deploy time.
  • ✔Ultra-lean agent: ~10MB footprint per node.
  • ✔Optional Cloudflare DNS: agents stay reachable even if the manager is down.
